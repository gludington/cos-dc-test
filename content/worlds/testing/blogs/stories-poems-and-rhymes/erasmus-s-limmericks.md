<p>There once was a man named stunk</p><p>Who drank lots of wine in his bunk</p><p>He was small with no legs</p><p>And cracked many eggs</p><p>And he did this without getting drunk</p><hr /><p>I once met a boy in a lair</p><p>While looking for a legend'ry hare</p><p>I asked for his name,</p><p>They were one and the same!</p><p>Didn't know that a hare could be were!</p><hr /><p>There once was a girl named Gorbacha</p><p>She could chew through chains like focaccia</p><p>She was a half orc</p><p>And liked to go "bork"</p><p>And she lived at her grandmother's dacha.</p><hr /><p>I once met a sentient vine</p><p>And though I'd hit a gold mine!</p><p>It looked like a thing</p><p>But man could it sing!</p><p>But the bones should have been a sign</p><hr /><p>I knew once of goblins, a pair</p><p>Together were seen everywhere</p><p>I'll get right to the point</p><p>By the hip they were joint!</p><p>At least until they met a bear.</p>
