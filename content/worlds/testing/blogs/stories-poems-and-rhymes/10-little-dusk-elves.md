<p dir="ltr" style="line-height:1.38;margin-top:12pt;margin-bottom:12pt"><span style="font-family:Arial, sans-serif"><em><strong style="font-weight:normal" id="docs-internal-guid-8508a7e5-7fff-d34d-fd81-af1836476a78">(accompanied by some ghastly imagery of dark-skinned, dark-haired male elven characters dying in many gruesome ways)</strong></em></span></p><p>10 little dusk elves</p><p>Thought they were just fine.</p><p>Their lord was a fool</p><p>And then there were nine.</p><hr /><p>9 little dusk elves</p><p>Exiled through the gate.</p><p>One died one the way,</p><p>And then there were eight.</p><hr /><p>8 little dusk elves</p><p>Under gypsy care.</p><p>Wagons roll'd on one</p><p>So's now seven there.</p><hr /><p>7 little dusk elves</p><p>Saw war at the fen.</p><p>Di'n't fight, one got fright</p><p>And there were six then.</p><hr /><p>6 little dusk elves</p><p>A witch caught one 'live.</p><p>Somday's mutton meat,</p><p>And then there were five.</p><hr /><p>5 little dusk elves</p><p>To the manor bore.</p><p>One’s lost ‘mong the dead</p><p>And then there were four.</p><hr /><p>4 little dusk elves</p><p>Hide in some trees.</p><p>A tree ate one good</p><p>And then there were three.</p><hr /><p>3 little dusk elves</p><p>Not sure what to do.</p><p>One’s not there at all</p><p>So it’s really two.</p><hr /><p>2 little dusk elves,</p><p>Daughter and a son.</p><p>One slew the other</p><p>Leaving only one.</p><hr /><p>1 lonely dusk elf,</p><p>Living in a hill.</p><p>Cried but has not died,</p><p>Someday surely will.</p><p><br /></p>
